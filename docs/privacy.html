Paper Moon

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Publisher: Raaru · Contact: raaruofficial@gmail.com · @raaruofficial

1. What the app collects

Nothing. Paper Moon has no accounts and no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The app does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data to the publisher or to anyone else.

2. Your play stays on your device

Gameplay runs fully offline and the app transmits nothing about your play. Sessions begin fresh at each launch; any leaderboard shown is your own session's finishes, on your own device only.

3. Wallet connection (optional)

If you choose to connect a Solana wallet, the app reads your wallet's public address so it can show your connection and check your rides and passes. What the app stores is kept only on your device, in its Keystore-encrypted store: a wallet connection token, so it can restore your own connection at the next launch (it is cleared when you disconnect); records of your purchases and pass entitlement; and records of your rookie-ride grants. None of it is transmitted to the publisher or to anyone else. Your private keys and funds never touch the app — every signature happens inside your own wallet app.

4. Purchases

Rookie rides are free: 3 on every install, no wallet needed, plus a separate one-time grant of 3 per signed-in wallet. The app collects nothing — no analytics, and nothing about your play is transmitted. The only times the app uses the network are wallet association through Mobile Wallet Adapter when you connect a wallet, and, when you initiate a purchase, a read against Solana mainnet RPC to verify that your payment settled. If you buy a day pass, payment is made in USDC on the Solana blockchain through your own wallet. Blockchain transactions are public by design. The app receives no card numbers, payment credentials, or personal information from a purchase.

5. Children

Paper Moon is a financial-history simulation game intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date before it applies to new downloads. Questions: raaruofficial@gmail.com or @raaruofficial.
