What it is

Paper Moon is a paper-trading arcade for the Solana Seeker. Every ride drops you at the start of a real, dated run from crypto market history — the chart plays out as it actually happened, and you decide when to get out. Your money is paper: $1,000 of it, with no deposits, no stakes, and nothing real on the line. The charts, the stories, and the verdicts are real.

How it works

Pick a scenario — the app carries 35 of them, each built from real historical charts and the news of its day. Size your ride, take profits or stop out as the candles run, and when you call time the ride ends with a verdict: EUPHORIA, TIME CALLED, or REKT. Sessions begin fresh at each launch and play runs fully on your device — no account, no sign-up.

Price

Your first three rookie rides are free — one claim per signed-in wallet, ever. After that, a day pass costs $3.50, paid in USDC on the Solana blockchain through your own wallet, and starts when your payment verifies on-chain. It covers 24 hours of rides. All sales are final. The app itself holds and moves no funds — every payment happens on-chain in your wallet app.

Nothing real is at stake

Paper Moon is a simulation. In-game balances, scores, and verdicts have no cash value, are not transferable, and cannot be redeemed. Nothing in the app is financial advice, an offer of any security or asset, or a promise of any return; historical charts are presented as history.
